## Where I am

One or two sentences. What branch, what state, what is half-finished.

## What's decided

Decisions made and why. Include alternatives ruled out — this is the part that
is most expensive to reconstruct later.

## What's next

Concrete next actions, in order. Enough that another agent could act without
asking questions.

## Open questions

What you could not resolve, and what you would need in order to resolve it.

## Dead ends

Approaches that did not work, so nobody repeats them.
